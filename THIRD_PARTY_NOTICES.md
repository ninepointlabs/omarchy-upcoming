# Third-party notices

Show search and episode air dates come from the public [TVmaze API](https://www.tvmaze.com/api).
No API key is used. Show names, networks, status, and episode titles are TVmaze data
and are rendered as plain text.

The producer-side stdout/stderr cap in `scripts/bounded-job-wrapper.sh` follows the
pattern used by ninepointlabs' Hermes Chat Omarchy plugin.
